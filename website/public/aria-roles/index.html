aria roles, in plain human

an aria role just tells a screen reader what kind of thing an element is — a button, a checkbox, a whole navigation menu — so it can describe it correctly, even when it's built from a plain <div>.

the tricky part: your design brain groups things by how they look. aria groups them by what they do. this guide is about that gap.
