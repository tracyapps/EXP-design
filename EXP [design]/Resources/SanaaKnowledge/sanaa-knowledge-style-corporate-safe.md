## TL;DR
The credible default: neutral surfaces, one trusted brand accent, consistent chrome, information density handled cleanly. Ask-for-it cues: "corporate", "professional", "safe", "enterprise", "B2B".

## Type pairing
- One workhorse sans (Inter, Roboto, Source Sans category) with real weight steps; maybe a second weight-class for data/mono contexts.
- Moderate scale (13/15/17/22/28 families); sentence case; tabular figures for data tables.

## Palette anchors
- White/light-gray surfaces (#FFFFFF, #F4F5F7), ink text (#1F2937 family), secondary gray (#6B7280 family), ONE brand accent used consistently for primary actions and links; semantic status colors kept conventional (green/amber/red families) and reserved for status.
- Conventional beats clever: if the brand guide exists, it wins; if not, blues/deep teals are the low-risk accent families.

## Spacing density
- Information-friendly: 8/12/16 inside components, 16/24 between sections; forms aligned to a strict column rhythm; tables compact but with clear row separation.
- Density uniformity matters more than airiness — sameness reads as reliability.

## Shape and corners
- Modest radii (4–8pt); 1pt neutral borders on inputs and cards; chrome is consistent and predictable; iconography one style, one weight.

## Shadow and texture
- Minimal: occasional soft shadow for true overlays (0 4 12 at ~8%); depth via surface steps and borders; no gradients, no texture.

## Motion temperament
- Functional only: 120–200ms fades for state changes; no decorative animation; loading states over spectacle.

## Where it works / where it fails
- Works: B2B SaaS, internal tools, finance/health/admin flows, anything audited or handed to many teams to maintain.
- Poor fit: brand-forward marketing, cultural/creative identities, standing out in consumer markets — "safe" is the feature and the ceiling.

## Adapting to existing tokens
- IF the document already has this shape THEN polish, don't restyle: consistency passes (spacing inventory, state coverage) beat novelty; tradeoff: less visible "improvement".
- IF the accent is unconventional THEN keep it as the sole accent and neutralize everything else; tradeoff: the brand carries the personality alone.
- IF the designer wants distinction inside safe THEN spend it on data presentation (tables, charts, empty states) rather than chrome.
