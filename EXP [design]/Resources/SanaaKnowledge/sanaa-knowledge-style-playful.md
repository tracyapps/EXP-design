## TL;DR
Energetic and personable: bright color, rounded shapes, expressive type moments, illustration-friendly chrome. Ask-for-it cues: "playful", "fun", "friendly", "consumer", "youthful".

## Type pairing
- A characterful sans or display face for headings (rounded, chunky, or quirky: Baloo, Poppins, Gantari category) + a plain legible sans for body so the personality stays in the headlines.
- Big heading moments, short lines; badges/tags get the expressive treatment; body stays disciplined.

## Palette anchors
- Bright multi-hue but structured: light neutral ground (#FFFDF7 family) or bold full-color ground, 3–5 saturated hues with assigned roles (one per section/theme), text in deep warm ink (#21201C family) rather than pure black.
- Gradients allowed as deliberate ramps between the document's own hues; color blocking replaces chrome.

## Spacing density
- Comfortable-medium: 12/16/20 paddings, cards breathe; rhythm can vary between sections (playful tolerates more variety than corporate) but each section stays internally consistent.

## Shape and corners
- Rounded everything (12–20pt) or mix of blob-adjacent shapes and circles for accents; borders rare — fills and contrast do the separation; squished/stretched elements only when the illustration system already does it.

## Shadow and texture
- Soft, colored shadows (shadow tinted with the element's hue, e.g. 0 6 0 of a deeper shade — "sticker lift") or flat with offset accents; optional subtle grain; no corporate gloss.

## Motion temperament
- Springy and quick: 150–250ms with overshoot on entrances, wiggle on success, micro-bounces on taps; animation expresses delight but respects Reduce Motion (static variants ready).

## Where it works / where it fails
- Works: consumer social/creative apps, education, food/retail, onboarding moments, community features.
- Poor fit: dense pro tools, long-form reading, audit-grade enterprise flows, contexts where credibility outranks charm (the energy needs constant editing).

## Adapting to existing tokens
- IF the document is neutral/corporate THEN localize playfulness to onboarding/empty states/celebrations and keep task flows quiet; tradeoff: split personality — name where each mode applies.
- IF the palette is single-hue THEN build the playful ramps from that hue's family plus one complementary; tradeoff: less rainbow, more cohesion.
- IF legibility suffers under bright fills THEN deepen text inks and pull fills back a saturation step — measured pairs (get_design_facts) decide, not enthusiasm.
