## TL;DR
Deliberately raw and loud: heavy borders, hard offset shadows, flat saturated color blocks, unapologetic type. Ask-for-it cues: "neo-brutalist", "brutalist", "bold", "raw", "anti-corporate".

## Type pairing
- One loud, confident face (heavy grotesque or expressive display: Archivo Black, Space Grotesk category) at large sizes; mono for labels/tags works well.
- Extreme size contrast; uppercase for impact lines; tight or negative tracking on display; body stays plain and legible (the loudness lives in headers/frames, not body text).

## Palette anchors
- Flat saturated blocks: warm paper or bold base (#FFFBEB, #FDE047, #FF6B4A families), ink-black borders/text (#000000), 2–5 flat hues max — no gradients, no tints.
- Off-palette neutrals (cream, mint, sky) as card fills; contrast between blocks is the composition.

## Spacing density
- Deliberately chunky: thick uniform gutters (16–24pt) with blocks butting edge-to-edge in places; overlap is allowed and should look intentional (offset, not accidental).

## Shape and corners
- 0–4pt radius; 2–4pt black borders on everything interactive; hard offset shadows (e.g. 4pt 4pt 0 #000) instead of blurs — the shadow is a drawn shape, not ambience.
- Stickers/badges: bordered pills, rotated slightly where it serves energy.

## Shadow and texture
- Hard shadows only (solid offset, no blur); optional halftone/noise fills for texture; no soft glows, no gradients.

## Motion temperament
- Snappy and physical: 100–150ms, springy one-step moves, hover states that translate the shadow (element "presses" into its offset); nothing floaty.

## Where it works / where it fails
- Works: indie products, event/creator brands, portfolios, campaigns, developer tools with personality.
- Poor fit: enterprise trust contexts, healthcare/finance body flows, long reading, accessibility-sensitive dense data (the weight and contrast games get loud fast).

## Adapting to existing tokens
- IF the document has a soft/corporate palette THEN keep the border+hard-shadow mechanics and mute the block fills to the document's hues; tradeoff: less shock, more wearable.
- IF radius tokens are rounded THEN drop to the smallest token radius rather than 0 to stay coherent; tradeoff: gentler attitude.
- IF the designer asks for "a little brutalist" THEN apply it to one region (hero, badges) and keep functional areas quiet — say where the loudness stops.
