## TL;DR
International Typographic Style: grid-ruled, type-led, asymmetric, content-first. Ask-for-it cues: "Swiss", "international style", "grid poster", "editorial-systemic".

## Type pairing
- One grotesque sans family doing all the work (e.g. Helvetica Now/Neue Haas category, Inter, Suisse category); weights (light/regular/medium) carry hierarchy — rarely a second family.
- Strong size steps on a modular scale; tight tracking on display sizes, neutral on body; sentence case; flush-left, ragged-right.
- Numbers/data set in the same face — tabular figures if available.

## Palette anchors
- Near-white surface (#FAFAF8), near-black text (#111111), one warm accent (classic red #E30613 family; adapt to the brand's accent) used sparingly for markers/rules.
- Lightness does the composing: mostly two extremes plus paper — few mid-tones. Grays for secondary text, not decoration.
- Accent appears as small marks (rules, bullets, labels), not large fills.

## Spacing density
- Airy but disciplined: generous outer margins (40–64pt), consistent gutter system, baseline-grid feel; content sits ON the grid, visibly.
- Gap scale from 8/16/24/40; whitespace is structural, not leftover.

## Shape and corners
- Sharp corners (0 radius) or minimal (2–4pt); hairline rules (1pt) as the main graphic device; no borders around everything — rules only where they carry structure.

## Shadow and texture
- None. Flat by conviction; layering is expressed through rules and position, not depth.

## Motion temperament
- Restrained: quick fades/offsets 150–200ms, ease-out, no bounce, no parallax theatrics.

## Where it works / where it fails
- Works: data-dense dashboards, docs, editorial systems, identity-forward marketing, anything with strong content hierarchy.
- Poor fit: playful consumer onboarding, emotional brand moments, contexts needing rich illustration or depth-heavy UI.

## Adapting to existing tokens
- IF the document accent is not red THEN keep the accent-as-small-mark discipline and swap the hue; tradeoff: some of the style's historic signal comes from the classic red.
- IF the document uses rounded corners widely THEN propose radius 2–4pt rather than 0; tradeoff: softer, less canonical.
- IF the typeface is fixed (no grotesque available) THEN keep the weight-step hierarchy and grid discipline — the system matters more than the face.
