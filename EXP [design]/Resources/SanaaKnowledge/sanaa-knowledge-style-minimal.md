## TL;DR
Reduced to what carries the task: few elements, generous space, quiet chrome, one accent. Ask-for-it cues: "minimal", "clean", "strip it back", "Apple-like".

## Type pairing
- One family, two or three weights; system-grade faces fit naturally (SF category, Inter). Size steps are moderate (not extreme poster contrast); sentence case; muted tracking.
- Hierarchy comes from size + color steps, rarely from bold-vs-bold.

## Palette anchors
- Neutral surfaces (#FFFFFF / #F5F5F7 light; #1C1C1E family dark), text near-black/near-white, ONE accent (#0A84FF family or brand accent) reserved for actions and links.
- Status colors appear only in status. No decorative color; grays are the second voice (#8E8E93 family).

## Spacing density
- Generous and consistent: 16/24/32 rhythm, large section breaks (40–64pt), content width constrained for measure.
- Density stays even across sibling sections — minimal breaks when space gets uneven, not when it gets tight.

## Shape and corners
- Soft, consistent radii (8–12pt cards, 6–8pt controls, or fully round for pills); 1px hairline separators used sparingly; borders rare — surfaces and space do the grouping.

## Shadow and texture
- Near-none; when depth is needed, one soft low-opacity shadow (e.g. 0 8 24 at 6–10%) or a surface-tone step instead. No noise, no gradients as decoration.

## Motion temperament
- Smooth, short (180–250ms), ease-in-out; subtle opacity/offset transitions; nothing springs or overshoots.

## Where it works / where it fails
- Works: productivity tools, settings, reading experiences, mobile-first flows, premium hardware companions.
- Poor fit: dense pro-tools needing chrome (audio/video editors), youth-brand energy, campaigns that need maximal expressiveness.

## Adapting to existing tokens
- IF the document is colorful THEN keep the neutral base and pull color out except the one accent — say what gets quieted; tradeoff: the design loses some energy.
- IF the document has heavy chrome (many borders) THEN replace grouping borders with surface steps + spacing; tradeoff: grouping reads subtler.
- IF content is genuinely dense THEN minimal becomes "calm density": keep the neutrality and consistency, relax the space targets.
