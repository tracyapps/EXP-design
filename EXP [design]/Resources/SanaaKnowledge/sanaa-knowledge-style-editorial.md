## TL;DR
Magazine logic on a screen: serif-led typography, strong headline hierarchy, asymmetric columns, captions and pull-quotes as design elements. Ask-for-it cues: "editorial", "magazine", "long-read", "publication".

## Type pairing
- A serif for headlines and long-form body (Georgia, Tiempo, Freight category; or a high-contrast display serif for titles), paired with a sans for labels/captions/UI chrome.
- Big type steps (headline 2.5–4× body); generous headline leading breathing room; drop caps or standfirst lines as structural devices.
- Italic for pull-quotes; small caps or tracked uppercase for kickers/labels.

## Palette anchors
- Paper-first: warm or neutral white (#FDFCF9), ink text (#1A1A1A), one restrained accent (deep red, forest, or navy family) for rules, links, and kickers.
- Mid-tones come from ink opacity steps (60–70%) rather than new hues. Images carry the color load.

## Spacing density
- Column-based: 2–3 columns with consistent gutters; text measures 45–75 characters; deliberate asymmetric grids (wide media column + narrow text column).
- Vertical rhythm from a baseline grid; pull-quotes and figures break the rhythm on purpose, clearly.

## Shape and corners
- Rectilinear, 0 radius; images full-bleed or hard-cropped; rules (0.5–1pt) above sections like magazine folios; no card chrome.

## Shadow and texture
- Essentially flat; depth comes from image treatment (duotone/full-color contrast) and type contrast. Optional paper-warmth in the surface tone.

## Motion temperament
- Slow-ish and literary: 250–400ms fades and slides for page-like transitions; nothing springy; media reveals once, then rests.

## Where it works / where it fails
- Works: long-form content, news/journals, brand storytelling, portfolios, documentation with narrative voice.
- Poor fit: transactional app UI (checkouts, settings), data-dense dashboards, quick-scan mobile task flows.

## Adapting to existing tokens
- IF the document has a sans-only token set THEN add the serif as a display/body-content role and keep sans for chrome — say which text runs switch; tradeoff: two families need discipline to stay coherent.
- IF the accent is bright/playful THEN deepen or desaturate it one step for print-like restraint; tradeoff: less pop, more continuity.
- IF the content is task-oriented UI THEN propose editorial treatment for narrative sections only, and keep functional areas in the document's existing language.
